05 / My approach

Understand first.
Measure second.
Build what’s needed.

A useful solution starts before the first line of code.

  1. Clarify the question

    What is the actual problem, and what decision needs to be made?

  2. Check the foundations

    What data and systems already exist? Can we trust them, and where are the constraints?

  3. Choose a practical solution

    Then decide what to measure, fix or build. Keep it understandable, maintainable and proportionate to the problem.
